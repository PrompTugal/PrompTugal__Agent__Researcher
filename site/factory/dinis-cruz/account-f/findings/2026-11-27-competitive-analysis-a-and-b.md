## Patterns across the sites (for cl-6.customers; part C is yours)
- Method and corrections are one click from the home page on the research-led sites (ProPublica, The Markup, Full Fact, Bellingcat, Texas Tribune), usually in the footer or main nav, with a named page for each.
- The strongest "show the work" pattern is a method piece paired with a story (The Markup's "How we investigated").
- Only Our World in Data leads its home page with volume counters; no site I read showed per-story source counts on the home page.
- Full Fact shows its own AI work in the main nav; Bellingcat publishes AI how-tos.
- The Portuguese sites show ownership and editorial line (ficha tecnica, estatuto, provedor) but I did not find method or corrections on their home pages.
