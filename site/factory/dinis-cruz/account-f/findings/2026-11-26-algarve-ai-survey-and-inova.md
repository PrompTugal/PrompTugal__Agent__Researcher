## 1. The AI adoption survey ("AI in Algarve Businesses")
Source: https://www.algarveevolution.pt/ai-in-algarve-businesses (English and Portuguese on the same page), read 2026-11-26.

- First wave of a survey the association says it repeats every six months. Collected 7 Oct to 12 Nov 2026. 89 businesses. Responses confidential and analysed in aggregate.
- The page's own caveat, which any use must repeat: respondents chose to take part, so the sample does not represent all Algarve businesses; 74% have fewer than 10 people and 39% are in accommodation; adoption is probably higher than the regional average. Groups compared have 11 to 66 businesses, so differences of a few points are not reliable.
- 56 of 89 (63%) use AI regularly or have it built in (27 built into processes, products or services, 29 regular use for some tasks); 20 (22%) only some staff on their own initiative; 13 (15%) do not use it or considered it and did not go ahead.
- Readiness: 82% say the team is open to AI, 75% that management knows where it can help; only 54% have rules on what may be entered into AI tools and only 38% have budget set aside for the next 12 months.
- Accommodation (35 businesses, 39% of the sample): 43% regular or built in, against 63% in other sectors and 100% of the 19 in consulting and technology; 10 of the 35 do not use it.
- 56 businesses (63%) want help to use (more) AI. Of those: 57% ask for hands-on team training, 52% an individual assessment, 41% a pilot project with UAlg, 36% help with funding applications. 35 of the 56 have no AI budget.

## 2. INOVA Algarve 3.0 (answers the open question in the places handover)
Source: https://www.algarveevolution.pt/inovaalgarve , read 2026-11-26.

- Project approved under the Collective Actions support system, promoted by a consortium of NERA, Algarve Evolution, Universidade do Algarve, Regiao de Turismo do Algarve, CCDR Algarve, KIPT and Tertulia Algarvia.
- Subject: AI and robotics applied to Algarve tourism companies; a digital transition programme.
- The free consulting call was for up to 15 SME tourism companies (a support service, not a grant or State aid). The page lists 15 selected, and a further 5 extra selected, so 20 in all. This settles "15 or 20": 15 were the call, 20 are listed as selected (two PDFs of selected applications linked from the page, not opened). Who leads the pilot is not stated on the page.
